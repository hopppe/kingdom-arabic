# Changelog

All notable changes to Kingdom Arabic will be documented in this file.

## [1.1.0] - 2026-01-15

### What's New
- **Audio playback** - Listen to Arabic pronunciation for words and verses
- **Google Translate button** - Quick access to translate selected text via Google Translate
- **Daily reminders** - Push notifications to remind you to study your flashcards
- **Search function** - Search through Bible verses to find specific passages

### Bug Fixes
- Various stability improvements and bug fixes

---

## [1.0.0] - Initial Release

### Features
- Arabic Bible reader with all 66 books
- Interactive word learning - tap any Arabic word to see its English translation
- Spaced repetition flashcard system (Anki-style algorithm)
- Session word management
- Flashcard groups for organization
- Offline-first - all content stored locally
